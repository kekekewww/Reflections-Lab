## Handling Research Gaps

`METADATA_ONLY`/`ABSTRACT_ONLY` do not suffice to support errors, performance figures, algorithmic details or measurement certifications in portions of papers that have not been read. These sources still have value for bibliographic and scope assessments; related implementations must be supported further by independent sources that have been read, clearly labeled derivations and V1–V5 validation. Historical papers whose full texts have not been read are not a reason to prevent the creation of analytical tests for image-source geometry, but passing analytical tests must not be described as reproducing the experiments in the original papers.
