## Completed

- Investigated from the user's observation that clicks follow path recalculation and change with reverb; the output buffer is not the cause: the three presets made no clear difference and 170 ms roughly doubled the clicks
- Retired paths now stay connected 100 ms past their fade, live and offline, so the native HRTF panner's tail is not cut
- Path direction and listener position now change over 40 ms; turning stays at 20 ms because longer ramps added dropouts without clearly fewer clicks; 100 ms was rejected for its extra dropouts

## Risks and unfinished work

- No person has listened to the fixed build; the numbers are signal measurements at 60 Hz, without the user's own scene or extensions
- Small clicks when turning are not fixed; no tested ramp removed them
- Volume dips of about 4-5 dB for up to about 30 ms around path changes remain; the one fade test was invalid
- One fixed run had 24 dropouts and another 6; old runs had occasional dropouts too, so a side effect is not ruled out at this sample size
- Retired paths hold the path budget longer, so a very busy scene could reach the limit sooner; not exercised
- The known limitations page is not updated; these limits are recorded in the document above
