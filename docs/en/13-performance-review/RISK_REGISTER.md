## Rules for reading evidence

- **CONFIRMED BY CODE** means a flow, limit or protection that can be inspected at the fixed baseline. It establishes neither cost magnitude nor audio quality.
- **CONFIRMED BY BROWSER PROFILE (historical)** refers to R17 receipts and their fixed sources/environment. It is not a retest of 05d40 or R21.
- **USER REPORTED** refers to R21 stutter. The tested bundle, output route, buffer and CPU load were not fully captured; the cause is unconfirmed.
- **HYPOTHESIS / NOT MEASURED** identifies a falsifiable mechanism or missing measurement. Late events, snapshot rejection and RAF delays cannot directly establish native output underruns.
- The initial R21 dirty implementation, regression repair and last observed bytes are different versions. Early short samples cannot be attributed to the final repair, and passing functional tests cannot establish smooth output. Version and hash boundaries follow the [audio review](AUDIO_GRAPH_DSP_REVIEW.md).

All priorities recommend future work. Short numbers such as `001` mean complete IDs such as `RLAB-TASK-PERF-20261101-001`. Every task remains BACKLOG, owner=null, with criteria=false and no automatic activation.

## Handling risks and stop conditions

Start with 001 and the baseline phase of 014, then use isolated, reversible, identical-load candidates for 002/003. Keep mechanisms HYPOTHESIS without positive evidence. An earlier onset that introduces latency, phase error or lost tails cannot be adopted solely for lower CPU. Leave native statistics unavailable where necessary; do not silently alter the product or add COOP/COEP to obtain them.

Recording preserves every accepted integer tick, event order, pre-state and cursor. High watermarks pause the producer rather than overwrite. Changes to formats, hashes, solver accuracy, authoritative dynamic physics or accepted ADRs stop being narrow optimizations and require separate compatibility/migration review. Missing devices, controlled inputs or actual-output evidence remain NOT MEASURED / NOT RUN. Research completion does not clear existing V1 gates.
